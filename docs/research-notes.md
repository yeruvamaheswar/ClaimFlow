# Public research background and source boundaries

ClaimFlow is an **independent portfolio project** informed by publicly discussed AI themes in claims, automation, enterprise knowledge retrieval, and governance. The product is **not based on access to USAA's internal tools or data**.

Starting points for further verification:

- USAA's [annual report](https://www.usaa.com/about/annual-report-members/) — claims-related business context.
- [USAA and Google Cloud vehicle-damage assessment case study](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/usaa-and-google-cloud-transform-insurance-operations) — earlier technical initiative; not evidence of today's internal architecture.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — voluntary risk-management guidance.
- [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-llm-applications-2025/) — application security background.
- [NAIC AI topic overview](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/artificial-intelligence) — insurance-sector governance context; not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Design opportunities described in this repo are **proposals**, not assertions that USAA has an unmet need, uses this technology stack, or would adopt the project. Review publication dates and verify links before citing them externally.
